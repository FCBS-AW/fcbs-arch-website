N° i.

How long have you been with Four Corners, and what first brought you to the company?

I’ve been with Four Corners for 18 months. It started with a simple lunch with Bowen Chapman that turned into a long conversation about where he wanted to take the window and door division. I’d spent years in custom fenestration, and the more we talked, the clearer it became that my experience lined up with what he was building. We both saw the fit right away, and I’ve been glad to be part of it ever since.

N° ii.

How long have you been in sales or the building industry?

Forty plus years. I’ve worked installation, sales, and ownership, so I’ve seen this business from every angle. I’ve been on the jobsite setting units, I’ve sat across the table from customers walking through options, and I’ve run a business where every decision came back to me. That background helps me anticipate problems before they happen and give honest answers about what will work and what won’t.

N° iii.

What’s your area of specialty, and which types of customers do you work with most?

Historic window and door replacement is where I do some of my most rewarding work. Older homes have a character you can’t fake, and the goal is to protect that while giving the homeowner the performance they need today. It takes patience, attention to detail, and real respect for the original craftsmanship. I also work with luxury homes, both new construction and renovation, along with boutique commercial projects. Whatever the project, I want the finished product to look like it belongs.

N° iv.

What do you enjoy most about helping builders, architects, and homeowners find the right products?

Bringing the whole project together. Every project has a lot of moving parts and a lot of people with their own priorities. I enjoy getting everyone on the same page, matching the vision across teams and product lines, and making sure the details are right, down to the hardware. Doing all of that while staying within budget is what makes a project successful, and seeing it come together at the end never gets old.

N° v.

What’s your favorite pastime outside of work?

Spending time with my wife. After forty years in this business, I’ve learned that time away from the job matters just as much as time on it, and there’s no one I’d rather spend it with.
