N° i.

How long have you been with Four Corners, and what first brought you to the company?

I’ve been with Four Corners for a little over a year. I joined for the chance to take on more responsibility, keep building my skills, and grow my career in the building industry.

N° ii.

How long have you been in sales or the building industry?

Most of my career has been in customer-facing roles, though I’m newer to outside sales. I’ve been in the building industry for nearly seven years, and the last five have been focused on windows and doors.

N° iii.

What’s your area of specialty, and which types of customers do you work with most?

Windows and doors, especially for new residential construction and remodeling. I work closely with builders, homeowners, and designers to take a project from concept through completion.

N° iv.

What do you enjoy most about helping builders, architects, and homeowners find the right products?

Helping customers work through the many available options and land on the solution that fits their needs. The best moment is when everything comes together and we find the right product for the project.

N° v.

What’s your favorite pastime outside of work?

Golf. If the weather’s nice on the weekend, you’ll probably find me squeezing in a round around Charleston.
