N° i.

How long have you been with Four Corners, and what first brought you to the company?

I joined Four Corners Building Supply in January 2022 as a sales coordinator. Since then I’ve held several roles, including service and installation management, before moving into sales. That experience gives me a clear view of what it takes to deliver a project from start to finish.

N° ii.

How long have you been in sales or the building industry?

I’ve been in the building supply industry for nearly five years. Before that, I worked in construction, renovations, roofing, and restoration, and spent several years in sales and marketing. Those years taught me how much I enjoy building relationships and solving problems alongside customers.

N° iii.

What’s your area of specialty, and which types of customers do you work with most?

I specialize in architectural windows and exterior doors, with a focus on multifamily, light commercial, and custom residential projects. I work closely with developers, general contractors, custom home builders, architects, and homeowners. Some customers know exactly what they need, and others want more guidance. I enjoy both, and I want my customers to know they can count on me.

N° iv.

What do you enjoy most about helping builders, architects, and homeowners find the right products?

I like bringing all the pieces of a project together. Every job has its own challenges, whether that’s finding the right product, staying within budget, or solving something unexpected. I enjoy the problem-solving, but what I value most is the relationships, and knowing I helped bring someone’s vision to life.

N° v.

What’s your favorite pastime outside of work?

I’m a foodie and love exploring Charleston’s restaurant scene. Friends and family often come to me for restaurant recommendations. I also enjoy time with my two daughters, the beach, and as much sunshine as I can get.
